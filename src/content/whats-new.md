## Cutouts

You can pin a live cutout of a webpage on the canvas! Just click 'edit' -> 'add cutout'.
Right now these are view-only on the canvas, best for creating a window into a dashboard or something. You can click the expand button to open it full-size right in PegBoard, or click the icon to open the full page.

## Invert hard-to-see icons

Some website icons are meant for light mode, so they use colors that are hard to see when you're in dark mode (github 😑). Now there's an "invert icon in dark mode" toggle in the link editor.

## Force light/dark mode

In settings -> appearance, you can now set light or dark mode independent from your system settings, or you can keep it to match your system!

Also added this as a configurable keyboard shortcut. The default ShadCN 'd' shortcut hurt my eyes a few times 🥲
